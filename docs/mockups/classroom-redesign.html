emoji_objects

The 3 states of matter can be distinguished at a macroscopic level:

Solid

Particles in fixed positions that vibrate. Interparticle forces are large.

Liquid

Particles further apart and free to move. Forces weaker than a solid.

Gas

Particles much further apart, moving freely. Forces are negligible.
